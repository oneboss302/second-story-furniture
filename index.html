The Process

From forgotten to refinished.

How every piece gets its second life.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Rescuing quality, solid wood furniture worth saving from estate sales, local finds, and curbsides.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    Stripping away worn finishes, repairing joinery, fixing hardware, and prepping wood down to its raw surface.

  3. 03

    Refinishing

    Applying high-grade stains, durable topcoats, or custom paint finishes by hand to bring out true character.

  4. 04

    Rehoming

    Posting finished pieces directly to Instagram as soon as they are complete and ready for a new home.
