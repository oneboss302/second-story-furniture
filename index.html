How a piece comes back

Four stages, every time. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is skipped.

  1. 01

    Strip

    Old finish, paint, and grime come off down to bare wood, so nothing is trapped under the new coat.

  2. 02

    Repair

    Loose joints reglued, veneer patched, hardware cleaned or replaced. Structure comes before shine.

  3. 03

    Sand

    Progressive grits smooth the surface and open the grain evenly, by hand on detail work.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Stain, oil, or paint applied in thin coats, sealed to hold up to daily use for years.

About the shop

Second Story Furniture Co. is a one-person refinishing shop based in the Philadelphia area. Every piece is stripped, repaired, and finished by hand — no shortcuts, no spray-booth assembly line.

Most work comes from pieces people already own and don't want to lose: a table with history, a dresser that's outlived three moves, something inherited that just needs its finish brought back. If it's solid wood and worth saving, it's worth doing right.
