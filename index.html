Process

How a piece comes back

Every found piece goes through the same four stages. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is skipped.

  1. 01

    Strip

    Old finish, paint, and grime come off down to bare wood, so nothing is trapped under the new coat.

  2. 02

    Repair

    Loose joints reglued, veneer patched, hardware cleaned or replaced. Structure comes before shine.

  3. 03

    Sand

    Progressive grits smooth the surface and open the grain evenly, by hand on detail work.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Stain, oil, or paint applied in thin coats, sealed to hold up to daily use for years.

About

About the shop

Second Story furniture is a one-person operation based in Springfield PA.

Every piece starts as someone else's curb alert, estate sale find, or free listing on Facebook Marketplace — then gets stripped, repaired, and finished by hand. No shortcuts, no spray-booth assembly line.

Nothing here is made to order. Each piece is one of a kind, refinished because it was worth saving, and posted once it's done. If solid wood and good bones are still under there, it's worth doing right.
